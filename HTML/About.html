About Mental Health

Mental health is an important part of our overall wellbeing. It influences how we think, feel and behave, how we manage stress, connect with others and make everyday decisions.

Everyone has mental health. It is just as important as physical health, and it can change throughout our lives. Mental health challenges can affect anyone, regardless of age, background or circumstances.

Looking after mental health does not mean feeling happy all the time. It means having ways to manage life's challenges, recognising when we need support and knowing where to find help.

Why Is Mental Health Important?

Everyday wellbeing

It can help us manage stress, learn, work and take part in daily activities.

Healthy relationships

Understanding our emotions can help us communicate and build supportive relationships.

Physical health

Mental and physical health are connected. Caring for one can support the other.

Getting support

Recognising challenges can help us reach out for appropriate help.

Breaking the Stigma

Stigma happens when people are judged or treated unfairly because of mental health challenges. It can make someone afraid to speak up or ask for help.

Remember: Mental health challenges are not a personal failure. Listen without judgement, avoid hurtful labels and encourage people to seek support.
