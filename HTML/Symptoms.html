Signs & Symptoms

Mental health challenges can affect people in different ways. A person may experience changes in thoughts, feelings, behaviour or usual routines. One sign on its own does not necessarily mean someone has a mental health condition.

Possible Signs to Look Out For

Emotional changes

  • Feeling sad, worried or overwhelmed for a long time
  • Becoming unusually irritable or angry
  • Losing interest in activities once enjoyed

Behaviour changes

  • Withdrawing from friends and family
  • Avoiding usual responsibilities
  • Noticeable changes in daily habits

Physical changes

  • Changes in sleeping patterns
  • Changes in appetite or energy
  • Frequent unexplained physical discomfort

Thinking changes

  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Feeling hopeless or unable to cope
  • Finding everyday decisions unusually difficult

How to Support Someone

  1. Choose a quiet moment and ask how they are doing.
  2. Listen patiently without interrupting or judging.
  3. Let them know their feelings matter and they are not alone.
  4. Encourage them to speak with a trusted adult, counsellor or healthcare professional.
  5. If they may be in immediate danger, seek urgent help from emergency or healthcare services.
Important: You do not have to diagnose or solve someone's problem. Help them connect with appropriate support.
